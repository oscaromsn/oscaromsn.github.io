A lawyer by training from Pontifical Catholic University of São Paulo (PUC-SP), I worked in renowned Brazilian law firms, acting in the most high-profile criminal cases across the country. As a programmer and data scientist, during the pandemic, I co-founded Pindograma, a journalism portal focused on public information, with a dedicated group of friends and colleagues. Together, we consolidated the largest Brazilian database of electoral research, which has been utilized by prestigious newspapers such as Folha de S. Paulo and Estadão.

Since early 2024, I have been dedicating myself to studying artificial intelligence almost daily, focusing on prompt engineering frameworks and specialized multi-agent system architecture (mainly DSPy and LangGraph).

Currently, I am an Innovator Fellow at the São Paulo University Innovation Center, where I conduct research and development on generative AI-powered solutions for the legal sector under the Innovation Learning Environment (NIDUS) initiative.

Main research areas:

- Agentic Systems Architecture
- AI Governance & Compliance
- AI Policy & Alignment
- Prompt Engineering
